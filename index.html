About Me

Hi! I’m Lauren, a third-year student at Northeastern University studying Data Science and Finance. I’m interested in how data can be used to understand problems, improve processes, and support better decisions. I enjoy the technical side of working with data, from programming and exploring datasets to building tools, but I’m just as interested in the business context behind the numbers and how insights can be put into practice.

Through my coursework and internships, I’ve had the opportunity to explore the intersection of technology, analytics, and finance. I’ve worked on projects involving data analysis, database management, and machine learning, and I’ve gained experience using tools like Python, SQL, R, and Excel. These experiences have helped me become a more thoughtful problem-solver and have encouraged me to keep exploring different ways technology can make work more efficient and accessible.

Outside of academics, I enjoy exploring Boston, trying new restaurants and attending community engagement events. Most recently, I was able to watch Wicked: The Musical at Citizens Opera House for a residence hall community event! I also enjoy music and karaoke, journaling, creating and editing videos, and spending time with family and friends.

I’m always looking for opportunities to learn and unlock parts of who I am. The next page details my home and background, and my contact information can be found at the bottom of every page. Thanks for stopping by!
